# 配置模板（骨架）

按你的接口名改。不要把真实账号贴进仓库。

```routeros
/system/identity/set name=R1
/interface/bridge/add name=bridge
/interface/bridge/port/add interface=ether2 bridge=bridge
/ip/address/add address=192.168.88.1/24 interface=bridge
/ip/pool/add name=dhcp_pool ranges=192.168.88.100-192.168.88.200
/ip/dhcp-server/add address-pool=dhcp_pool interface=bridge name=dhcp1
/ip/dhcp-server/network/add address=192.168.88.0/24 gateway=192.168.88.1 dns-server=192.168.88.1
/ip/dns/set servers=1.1.1.1 allow-remote-requests=yes
/ip/firewall/nat/add chain=srcnat out-interface=pppoe-out1 action=masquerade
```

拨号、防火墙细节见对应实战课。
